# Data card

This directory holds the **data card** for the manuscript:

- [`0NDATACARD_C5.md`](0NDATACARD_C5.md), written in Chinese and generated by
  `code/120datacard_sha256.py`. Section 1 describes each of the three real data sets: its role
  in the paper, source, how it was obtained or extracted, licence, path, size and full SHA-256
  digest. Section 2 lists the registered derived files, that is the result files behind the
  paper's numbers and a few bookkeeping records, each with its content, size and the first 16
  hex digits of its SHA-256. Section 3 states how the card
  corresponds to the manuscript, Section 4 lists missing files, and Section 5 is the reverse
  coverage check over the result directories. The full digests of all files are in
  [`../data/SHA256-MANIFEST.tsv`](../data/SHA256-MANIFEST.tsv) and
  [`../outputs/120datacard_sha256.json`](../outputs/120datacard_sha256.json).

No new data were collected for this work: every real system is previously published
third-party data under CC BY 4.0, obtainable from the DOIs listed in
[`../data/DATA.md`](../data/DATA.md). The two literature tables are shipped as extractions in
`../data/processed/pmc_real/`; the large avocado xlsx is referenced through its Mendeley DOI and
is not re-hosted. There is no restricted or self-collected data in this project.

The derived result files backing each number in the paper *are* shipped, with
their SHA-256 digests; regenerate the digests with
`python code/120datacard_sha256.py`.
